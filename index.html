About Me

I am a Ph.D. candidate in the Department of IT Fusion Technology at Chosun University, Republic of Korea. My research lies at the intersection of affective computing, physiological signal processing, and personalized AI for healthcare.

My recent work focuses on depression detection from facial videos and ECG signals, including knowledge distillation and personalized calibration that adapt models to individual users. I have also worked extensively on cuffless blood pressure estimation and waveform reconstruction from ECG/PPG signals.

I am open to collaborations and postdoctoral opportunities — feel free to contact me by email.
