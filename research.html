Research

My research develops AI methods that turn facial behavior and physiological signals into reliable, personalized health assessments.

Depression & Mental Health Assessment

Detecting depression and its severity from facial videos and ECG signals, and adapting general models to each individual through knowledge distillation and continual personalized calibration. Related work also covers mental stress detection from ECG and PPG.

Representative papers
  • KBS 2026 Personalized calibration for depression detection using knowledge-distilled facial video models
  • NEUCOM 2026 DepSTDSAM: Spatio-temporal dual-sparse attention model for depression prediction based on facial behavioral structure representations
  • NEUCOM 2025 STSFF-net: a two-stream network for depression detection from facial expressions
  • ITAIC 2025 Multi-Task Learning with Inception Network for ECG-Based Depression Severity Prediction and Classification
  • ITAIC 2025 A Dual-pass Channel-attention Convolutional Model for Depression Detection in the Wild Video and Video Blogs
  • BIBM 2024 Building an explainable deep residual network for depression detection from ECG signals
  • BIBM 2024 Mental stress detection using PPG signals based on transformer-LSTM model
  • Sensors 2021 Mental stress classification based on a support vector machine and naive Bayes using electrocardiogram signals

Cuffless Blood Pressure & Waveform Reconstruction

Continuous, cuffless blood pressure estimation and arterial blood pressure (ABP) waveform reconstruction from ECG and PPG using machine learning and deep networks (CNN-LSTM, 1-D SENet, U-Net), plus reconstruction of clinical 12-lead ECG from reduced leads.

Representative papers
  • KBS 2024 Reconstruction of arterial blood pressure waveforms based on squeeze-and-excitation network models using electrocardiography and photoplethysmography signals
  • ESWA 2024 Development of continuous cuffless blood pressure prediction platform using enhanced 1-D SENet–LSTM
  • ASOC 2023 Cascade forest regression algorithm for non-invasive blood pressure estimation using PPG signals
  • IEEE Access 2025 Evaluating time-series deep learning models for accurate and efficient reconstruction of clinical 12-lead ECG signals
  • ICIS 2023 Reconstruction of ABP waveform from the ECG or PPG signals using enhanced 1-D UNetwork
  • ICAIC 2023 Cuff-less blood pressure estimation from ECG and PPG using CNN-LSTM algorithms
  • AIKE 2022 Machine learning algorithm for non-invasive blood pressure estimation using PPG signals

ECG Analysis & Wearable Sensing

Fast and lightweight models for ECG-based arrhythmia and heart disease classification, and stretchable textile-based triboelectric sensors for wearable applications.

Representative papers
  • Appl. Sci. 2022 Lightweight ensemble network for detecting heart disease using ECG signals
  • AIKE 2022 Development of a Variety of Fast Machine Learning Model for ECG-based Arrhythmia Classifier
  • RSC Adv. 2023 Fully stretchable textile-based triboelectric nanogenerators with crepe-paper-induced surface microstructures
